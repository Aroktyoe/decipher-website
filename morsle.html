Rules

Use the . and - keys or buttons to type morse, and press the Enter key to input the letter equivalent.


Try to guess the target word in as few tries as possible.


Green: the letter guessed is in the word at the correct place.

Yellow: the letter guessed is in the word at the wrong place.

Gray: the letter guessed is not in the word at all.


Advanced users can hide the morse translations for another challenge!
